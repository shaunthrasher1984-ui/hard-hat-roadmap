About Shaun Thrasher

OHS professional and college instructor with 20+ years in construction and industrial work, helping people get hired and move up in health & safety and construction management.

My background

I spent more than 20 years on the tools and on job sites, as a construction foreman, heavy equipment operator and maintenance technician, before moving into occupational health and safety. In 2023 I earned my Occupational Health and Safety diploma with honours. I work as a Health & Safety Advisor on heavy civil and industrial sites, doing inspections, orientations, incident investigations and audits. Today I'm a college instructor in OHS and construction management, and I also work as a safety consultant and trainer.

I also know workplace injury from the inside. I've lived more than 11 years of first-hand WSIB experience as an injured worker, recovering from two traumatic brain injuries and a fractured pelvis, and going through three separate return-to-work and reintegration processes. That experience shapes how I teach and consult. Safety isn't paperwork to me; it's what gets people home, and when someone does get hurt, knowing your rights and the process makes all the difference.

Along the way I've watched a lot of capable people get stuck. They don't know which training matters, how to present their experience, or what the next step looks like. Good career guidance shouldn't depend on who you know or what you can afford. That's why I built Hard Hat Roadmap: free career roadmaps, training and tools for people in OHS and construction management. Whether you're just starting out or going after your next role, my goal is to help you get hired, keep growing and get home safe.
