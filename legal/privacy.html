1. Who we are

Hard Hat Roadmap (the “Service”) is operated by Shaun Thrasher, an individual in Grand Falls, New Brunswick, Canada (“we,” “us,” or “Owner”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use the Service.

Site: https://shaunthrasher1984-ui.github.io/hard-hat-roadmap/

2. Scope and Canadian privacy principles

We aim to handle personal information in a manner consistent with the principles of Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, provincial private-sector privacy laws. In short: we collect what we need, tell you why, use it for those purposes, keep it reasonably secure, and respect your rights to access and correct it.

3. What the Service is today

Hard Hat Roadmap is currently a largely static / client-side career and practice resource (roadmaps, jobs listings presentation, and “Test Your Skills” practice quizzes). Much browsing can occur without creating an account.

We expect to add features over time (accounts, saved progress, paid unlocks via Stripe). This Policy describes both current and planned collection so you know what to expect.

4. Information we may collect

4.1 Information you provide

  • Contact messages — if you email us or use a contact form: name, email address, and message content;
  • Account details (when accounts are offered) — email, display name, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), preferences, and progress you choose to save;
  • Payment-related details (when paid features launch) — billing email, and limited transaction metadata. Full card numbers are handled by the payment processor (expected: Stripe), not stored by us.

4.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical / log data — IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and approximate timestamps, as recorded by our hosting provider (e.g., GitHub Pages) or future application servers;
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 6;
  • Analytics (placeholder) — if we enable privacy-respecting analytics, we may collect aggregated usage metrics (pages, referrers, approximate location). We will update this Policy and, where required, obtain consent before enabling non-essential analytics.

5. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Respond to your inquiries;
  • Create and manage accounts and saved progress (when offered);
  • Process payments and prevent fraud (when paid features launch);
  • Understand aggregate usage and fix bugs;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use;
  • Communicate service-related notices (e.g., security or material policy changes).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Service may use:

  • Essential / functional storage — e.g., theme preference, track selection (OHS vs construction management), or session state needed for the site to work;
  • Account / auth cookies — when accounts are added, to keep you signed in securely;
  • Analytics cookies — only if/when we enable analytics (optional; will be disclosed and controlled appropriately).

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Disabling essential storage may limit features.

7. Payment processors

When paid features launch, payments will be processed by a third party (expected: Stripe). Stripe’s handling of card data is governed by Stripe’s own privacy policy and PCI-DSS obligations. We receive confirmation of payment and limited billing metadata needed for records and support — not your full card number.

8. Service providers and hosting

We may use service providers to host the site, send email, process payments, or provide analytics. Examples may include GitHub (Pages hosting), Stripe, and email or auth providers. Providers process information on our instructions and are expected to protect it appropriately. Some providers may process data outside Canada (e.g., in the United States); where that occurs, we will take reasonable contractual and organizational steps consistent with Canadian expectations for cross-border processing.

9. Retention

  • Contact emails / form messages — kept as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterward (typically up to 24 months unless a longer period is needed for disputes or legal compliance);
  • Account data — while your account is active, and for a reasonable period after deletion or inactivity (to be defined when accounts launch; often 30–90 days for backup purge, longer if required by law or billing);
  • Payment records — retained as required for tax and accounting (commonly several years under Canadian recordkeeping rules);
  • Server / security logs — typically short periods unless needed for security investigation.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small indie product (HTTPS, access controls on systems we operate, hashed passwords via auth providers when accounts exist). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a unique password and protect your devices.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (without affecting prior lawful processing);
  • Request deletion of account or certain personal information, subject to legal retention needs (e.g., invoices);
  • Challenge our compliance with this Policy.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by law. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial privacy commissioner if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

12. Children

The Service is aimed at adults preparing for career and professional designations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. For material changes, we will provide notice on the Service or by email where appropriate. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
